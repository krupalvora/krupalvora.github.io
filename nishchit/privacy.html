Nishchit privacy policy

Covers the Nishchit website and the Nishchit browser extension. Last updated 22 November 2026.

The short version

Nishchit does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.

What the extension does

  • When you search a train on the Nishchit website, the extension fetches that train's public route and reservation chart from www.irctc.co.in, from your own browser, and hands the result to the Nishchit page.
  • It only ever calls three public chart endpoints (train schedule, train composition, coach composition), and only when a Nishchit page asks.
  • If IRCTC needs its chart page open to answer, the extension opens IRCTC's chart page in a background tab and closes it after a couple of idle minutes.
  • When it is first installed, it opens the Nishchit website once so you can see it worked.
  • It does not read your IRCTC account, bookings, passwords or any other site. It cannot see other tabs.

What is sent where

Your search (train number, date, stations, class) goes from your browser to IRCTC, as it would if you looked up the chart on IRCTC yourself. Nothing is sent to Nishchit or to anyone else. Search details stay in the page address so you can share or bookmark a search.

Permissions

  • Access to www.irctc.co.in: to fetch the public chart.
  • Scripting: to make the chart request from an IRCTC tab when IRCTC requires it.
