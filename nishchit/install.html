Add Nishchit to your browser

IRCTC only shares the live chart with your own browser, so Nishchit needs a small free extension to read it. It takes about two minutes, once.

  • Free
  • No account
  • No tracking
  • Works only on Nishchit

Step 1 of 5

Download the extension

It's a small zip file (15 KB) that goes to your Downloads folder.

Download Nishchit extension

Step 2 of 5

Unzip it and keep the folder

Open your Downloads folder and unzip nishchit-extension-chrome.zip.

Keep the nishchit-extension folder. Move it to Documents if you like, but don't delete it: your browser runs the extension from there.

How to unzip on other computers
  • Mac: double-click the zip.
  • Windows: right-click the zip → Extract All… → Extract.
  • ChromeOS: open the zip in Files, then copy the folder into My files.
  • Linux: unzip nishchit-extension-chrome.zip

Step 3 of 5

Open the extensions page

Browsers don't let websites link to this page, so copy the address and paste it into a new tab.

chrome://extensions
  1. Open a new tab: Ctrl T
  2. Paste: Ctrl V
  3. Press Enter

Step 4 of 5

Turn on Developer mode

It's a switch in the top-right corner of the extensions page.

Extensions Developer mode

This only lets you add extensions from a folder. It doesn't change anything else.

Step 5 of 5

Click “Load unpacked” and pick the folder

A button row appears under the title. Click Load unpacked, choose the nishchit-extension folder, then click Select.

That's it. Nishchit opens in a new tab with “You're all set” when the extension is in.

Stuck?

I don't see “Load unpacked”

Developer mode is still off. Turn it on (step 4) and the button row appears.

It says “Manifest file is missing”

You picked the zip, or the folder that contains the folder. Pick the nishchit-extension folder itself: the one with manifest.json directly inside.

Updating to a new version

Download again, replace the old nishchit-extension folder with the new one, then click the reload arrow ↻ on Nishchit's card on the extensions page.

Removing it

On the extensions page, click Remove on Nishchit's card, then delete the folder.

Other browsers and devices

Firefox Coming soon

Firefox only installs add-ons signed by Mozilla. Nishchit is being submitted to Firefox Add-ons; once approved it installs in one click, on computers and Android.

Android Coming soon

Chrome on Android has no extensions. Nishchit will work in Firefox for Android once it's on Firefox Add-ons. For now, use a computer or check the chart on IRCTC.

iPhone and iPad Not available

Extensions there only come from the App Store. Use a computer, or check the chart on IRCTC.

Safari on Mac Not available

Safari extensions have to come from the App Store. On a Mac, use Chrome, Edge or Brave with the steps above.

What the extension does

  • When you search on Nishchit, it fetches that train's public chart from IRCTC, from your own browser.
  • It works only on the Nishchit website. It can't see your IRCTC login, bookings or other tabs.
  • If IRCTC only answers from its own chart page, the extension opens that page in a background tab, which closes itself after a couple of minutes.
  • Nothing is collected or sent anywhere except IRCTC. Privacy policy
