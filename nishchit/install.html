browser extension

Get live charts
in your browser.

IRCTC blocks servers from reading the chart, but it answers your own browser. The free Nishchit extension reads the chart from your browser and hands it to Nishchit. No account, no tracking.

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi

Mac · Windows · Linux · ChromeOS

Download extension (.zip)

Free · 15 KB · source code

  1. Unzip it. You get a folder called nishchit-extension.
    • Mac Double-click the zip in Downloads.
    • Windows Right-click the zip → Extract All… → Extract.
    • Linux unzip nishchit-extension-chrome.zip
    • ChromeOS Open the zip in Files, then copy the nishchit-extension folder into My files.
    Move the folder somewhere it can stay, like Documents. The browser loads the extension from it, so don't delete it.
  2. Open your browser's extensions page. Paste this into the address bar: chrome://extensions
  3. Turn on Developer mode. It's a switch in the top-right corner.
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the nishchit-extension folder.
  5. Reload this page. The box at the top turns green. Your searches then show Live · your browser.
Updating to a new version

Download the zip again and replace the old nishchit-extension folder with the new one. Then, on the extensions page, click the reload arrow ↻ on Nishchit's card.

Removing it

On the extensions page, click Remove on Nishchit's card, then delete the folder.

On iPhone and iPad, browser extensions can only come from the App Store, so Nishchit can't offer one there yet. Use Nishchit on a computer, or check the chart on IRCTC directly.

Safari on Mac

Not available

Safari extensions have to come from the App Store. On a Mac, use Chrome, Edge or Brave with the download above.

What the extension does

  • When you search on Nishchit, it fetches that train's public chart from IRCTC, from your own browser.
  • It works only on the Nishchit website and only reads the public chart. It can't see your IRCTC login, bookings or other tabs.
  • Sometimes IRCTC only answers from its own chart page. Then the extension opens that page in a background tab, which closes itself after a couple of minutes.
  • Nothing is collected or sent anywhere except IRCTC. Privacy policy
