How Nishchit finds you a seat

  1. 1
    Pick your train and stations.

    Works once IRCTC has prepared the chart, usually a few hours before departure.

  2. 2
    We read the live chart, coach by coach.

    Every berth that is free for any part of your trip shows up, grouped by coach.

  3. 3
    Get a plan you can book.

    One berth for the whole trip, or a few berths stitched together with safe switches at long halts.

Not affiliated with IRCTC or Indian Railways. A plan is not a ticket.

Chart data comes live from IRCTC through your own browser. Fares are estimates. Always verify on IRCTC before travelling.
